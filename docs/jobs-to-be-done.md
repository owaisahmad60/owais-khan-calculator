# Jobs To Be Done

Each job describes what the person needs to get done whether or not this app exists. No job names a button, a screen or a feature of the app.

---

## Loan Applicant (R-1)

**J-1 (Loan Applicant).** "When I am considering borrowing money for a large purchase, I want to know what I would have to pay every month, so I can decide whether it fits within my monthly budget."

**J-2 (Loan Applicant).** "When a lender has offered me a loan, I want to understand the total amount I would repay over its whole life, so I can judge how much the borrowing really costs me and whether it is worth it."

**J-3 (Loan Applicant).** "When I am choosing between different loan terms or different offers, I want to see how each option changes my payments and my total cost, so I can pick the one that suits my situation best."

**J-4 (Loan Applicant).** "When I am committing to a loan that will run for years, I want to know how much of each payment goes to interest and how much reduces what I owe, so I can understand how quickly my debt is actually going down."

---

## Loan Officer (R-2)

**J-5 (Loan Officer).** "When a customer asks 'what if' questions during a meeting, I want to get an accurate answer straight away for each new set of figures, so I can keep the conversation moving without working the figures out by hand."

**J-6 (Loan Officer).** "When I have agreed a set of loan figures with a customer, I want them to have a clear record of what I quoted, so they can review it later and there is no dispute about what was said."

**J-7 (Loan Officer).** "When I quote figures to a customer, I want to be sure the numbers I give are correct and consistent with each other, so I do not give wrong information that the customer relies on."

**J-8 (Loan Officer).** "When I go through figures with a customer on a screen, in surroundings that may be bright or dim, I want the figures to be comfortable to read, so I can keep the customer's attention on the numbers and not on the screen."
